How it works

Four steps from raw transactions to a decision you can act on today.

01

Connect

Sync Zoho Books with one click, or upload a bank statement CSV export — no technical setup required.

02

Analyze

Eleven specialist skills compute your health score, cash flow risk, concentration risk, and anomalies deterministically.

03

Understand

Your configured AI provider narrates the findings in plain English — grounded only in the numbers already computed, never guessing.

04

Act

Action Center turns findings into owned, dated tasks. Export board-ready executive reports in one click.

Skill-configured, not hard-coded

Every skill is a plain-language spec your team can edit — thresholds, weights, and tone — with no code changes.
